About Bazar

Everyday Essentials, Made Easier.

Bazar is a new online store from clickfor.in for the things you use every day. We’re getting ready to open, and we’re building it carefully, one product at a time.

B
Built together, one product at a time

Bazar is being built by a group of people who share one goal. Everyone in the team looks for new products and suppliers, compares prices and tries samples. A product joins the Bazar list only after it has been checked and we are sure it is worth it.

Our first range is still taking shape, with a soft spot for traditional Kerala favourites. We’ll keep adding what works and leave out what doesn’t.

  • Good quality, fair prices

    We look for products that are good and priced fairly.

  • Made to last

    We choose items with a good shelf life, so they stay fresh in your kitchen.

  • Packed to travel well

    We pick products that store and travel safely, so less gets damaged along the way.

  • Worth buying again

    We want Bazar to be full of things you’ll come back for.

  • More convenient

    Where newer, smarter products make life easier, we want to bring them to you.

With everyone’s help and support, we’ll grow together.
